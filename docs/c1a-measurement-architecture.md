## Contract and sequencing
Use canonical feet for coordinates and dimensions (pitch rise per 12). Derive SF, LF, CY and EA from geometry; retain full precision. Persist source entities, not calculated totals. Shared project settings/rates are project-owned; dimensions explicitly entered on entities do not change with defaults. Specialist entities are discriminated by kind and belong to a matching workspace. Quantities must have stable workspace/entity/physical identity and quantity code, unit and basis. Missing input and unquantified are not zero. Distinguish assignment from calculated completeness; no assignment alone certifies measured scope. Exclusion needs explicit reason. Duplicate physical quantity identities must block contribution; no automatic cross-sheet spatial registration or engineering design.

## Work packages
1. Pure geometry/topology and measurement engine. Simple concave polygons permitted; reject self-intersection, degenerate edges, opening outside parent or overlapping openings, shared-edge duplicates/partial overlaps, unsupported plane overlaps. No automatic union or 3D reconstruction. Distinguish missing versus explicit zero pitch.
2. Roof-specific canvas interaction and toolbar, selection/inspector, snapping, pan/zoom, shared underlay/calibration, independent workspace state. Transient previews do not mutate document; commit once per operation.
3. Edge classification eave/rake/ridge/hip/valley/abutment/parapet/unclassified, one or two parent planes, endpoints on referenced boundaries, compatible directional rise; if both elevations known verify meeting heights; absent elevation report junction not fully verified. Opening polygons trace parent, explicit deduction treatment. Applicability is separate from classification; unknown not zero, exclusion explicit. Protect parent deletion.
4. Independent horizontal/sloped soffit and insulation polygons, assembly type, explicit thickness for volume, raw quantity inspector. Gross projected shoelace; sloped area = projected * hypot(1,pitch/12); net = gross minus nonoverlapping included openings. Edge deltaZ = -pitch/12*(dx*cos(theta)+dy*sin(theta)); length = hypot(dx,dy,deltaZ). Board feet = actual SF * depth inches. SQ = SF/100 display conversion only. No procurement threshold/waste/rate.
5. Canonical persisted specialist entities, undo/redo includes geometry and measurements without rewinding shared settings, restore and export/import, isolated workspace changes, full CI and local acceptance. Preserve existing 37 tests.

### R2B feasibility decision (2026-11-24)

CGAL Straight_skeleton_2 is a GPL package with commercial licensing alternative: https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Straight_skeleton_2/group__PkgStraightSkeleton2Ref.html and https://www.cgal.org/license.html . Browser-compatible WASM wrappers exist (https://github.com/StrandedKitty/straight-skeleton and https://github.com/matthewjacobson/str8), but wrapper licensing does not resolve underlying CGAL obligations. Native JS alternatives such as https://github.com/mflux/straightskeleton require independent robustness evaluation. No dependency installation, licensing purchase, browser benchmark or production qualification performed. Recommend deferring general concave skeleton generation; preserve manual polygon workflow and explicit rectangular-only generator. Any subsequent prototype requires license review, deterministic fixtures for L/T/U outlines, simultaneous split/collapse events, near-collinear edges, reversed winding, polygon coverage without overlaps, unique edge identity and compatible elevation, bounded runtime and deployment checks. No approximate fallback. Owner to confirm deferral at final acceptance. No merge without authorization.

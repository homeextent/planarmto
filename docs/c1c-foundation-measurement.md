### R1 reference footprint contract
Pad and slab footprint SF are per-component non-additive reference records in `measureFoundation().footprintReferences`, separate from the material contribution rows. Include stable element ID, code, role reference, gross SF, exclusion SF, net SF and pending reason. Valid confirmed poured-concrete geometry can establish area without thickness; potential unresolved physical overlaps withhold net area. Slab openings deduct area, thickening does not. Inspector displays selected component reference only; no implicit project area sum or pricing. C.1E/C.2 must retain this distinction.

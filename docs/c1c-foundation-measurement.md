## Geometry and quantities
Canonical feet. Source geometry and stable entity/physical/assembly IDs authoritative; separate group identity; derive quantities on load. SF LF CY EA SQ BF remain shared units; CF informational or intermediate only. Isolated wall L*t*h/27; strip L*w*d/27; pad/slab/region net polygon area*depth/27. Actual wall and strip footprints with joined corners and deterministic nonoverlapping attribution, no blanket length corrections. Reconcile vertical intervals where relevant; isolated quantities need no absolute datum, ambiguous overlapping contributions pending. Reject duplicates even with distinct IDs; compatible adjacent pieces allowed, distinct confirmed vertical/material layers may coexist. Missing dimensions never zero. Explicit construction type and source, no structural sizing. Wall openings parent, along-wall position, width, height, vertical offset; validate containment and no overlap, refuse unsupported intersections. Slab openings explicit; thickening total depth subtracts existing slab depth, additional depth not deducted twice. Pad count EA, reference footprint SF. Gross/net/deduction separately displayed, no double pricing of reference quantities.
Excavation polygons or explicit trench width/depth; reconcile intersections; in-situ volume only. Backfill explicit disjoint regions/intervals, no inferred basement void. Granular installed geometric volume, material/layer specified, no density/tonnage/compaction conversion. Horizontal insulation explicit polygon or confirmed slab subset, vertical insulation actual face length*coverage height; material/thickness, foam BF=SF*inches; no R-values. Waterproofing independent lines or confirmed actual wall face, explicit coverage height, material and opening treatment; separate specified systems allowed. Drainage independent lines or bounded exterior perimeter with explicit offset, horizontal plan LF only; no automatic fittings or slope. Distinct material layers must have traceable identities. Exclusions require reason; included/excluded/pending independent of assignment. Partial known subtotal with pending reasons; wholly pending not zero.

### R1 reference footprint contract
Pad and slab footprint SF are per-component non-additive reference records in `measureFoundation().footprintReferences`, separate from the material contribution rows. Include stable element ID, code, role reference, gross SF, exclusion SF, net SF and pending reason. Valid confirmed poured-concrete geometry can establish area without thickness; potential unresolved physical overlaps withhold net area. Slab openings deduct area, thickening does not. Inspector displays selected component reference only; no implicit project area sum or pricing. C.1E/C.2 must retain this distinction.
